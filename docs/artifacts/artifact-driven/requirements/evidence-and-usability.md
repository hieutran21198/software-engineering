# Evidence and small-change authoring

## Need

Authors need durable evidence that does not depend on one English sentence, a clear publication reconciliation procedure, and a short starting structure for small outcomes. Existing phase approval, source ownership and merge obligations still apply.

## Acceptance criteria

- A1: Equivalent human-readable evidence descriptions produce the same interpretation when their explicit structured facts agree. Failed, incomplete, missing, conflicting and stale evidence remains visible. An evidence record never grants approval or proves a merge by itself.
- A2: Publication review identifies the selected version, exact originating delivery, required implementation scope and supporting merge evidence. Missing proof prevents a verified completion claim. Current root declarations can be reconciled without editing established snapshots.
- A3: A single-artifact, one-task outcome has a copyable minimal scaffold containing all required unit, change, phase, task and evidence fields. Authors reference evidence once instead of repeating its content. No new mandatory service or workflow database is required.
- A4: Existing prose-only artifacts remain readable. New invalid explicit records produce actionable diagnostics and cannot silently fall back to a positive legacy claim.
- A5: Canonical guidance, embedded bundle, role guidance, examples and adoption tests agree. Documentation respects parent-to-child links. Existing five phases and explicit plan approval remain.

## Impact and self-check

Artifact Plane consumes the new contract. The ADD change-delivery, version-publication and unit-validation journeys are affected; ownership and membership do not create new dependency edges. Requirements distinguish recorded results from independent verification. KISS keeps evidence in existing files; STE uses direct criteria. Self-check: passed.
