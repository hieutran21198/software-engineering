# Gameplay Content

Own playable treatments under [Game Design](../core/game-design-foundation.md) and applicable [Setting Rules](../core/setting-rules.md). Fictional identity remains in [World](world.md).

| Category | Record | Linked inputs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Enemies | Behavior, tells, counters, reward and challenge role. | Fictional actor when grounded; supported actions and encounter context. |
| Bosses | Phases, counterplay, escalation, stakes and payoff. | Actor, narrative conflict, abilities and encounter. |
| Weapons | Actions, costs, acquisition, effects, balance and feedback. | Fictional object, supported verbs and relevant spatial context. |
| Items | Uses, acquisition, scarcity, effects and meaningful feedback. | Object identity and supporting systems; story conditions where needed. |
| Abilities | Conditions, limits, costs, unlocks and feedback. | Fictional capability and actor; traversal rules if movement changes. |

Prototype with placeholders after applicable system contracts exist. Finalize with identity, consequences and relevant interaction tests. A new action requires Core review rather than being hidden inside an item.

**Check:** Does each treatment use supported actions and produce coherent feedback/consequences while preserving its fictional identity?
