# Level Content

Own particular playable spaces and placed events under [Level Design](../core/level-design-foundation.md) and applicable fictional, game and narrative constraints.

| Category | Record | Linked inputs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Levels | Layout, routes, entries/exits, landmarks, objectives, pacing and placements. | Fictional location, supported traversal/actions, relevant quests, encounters and visual cues. |
| Encounters | Fight, puzzle, conversation or set piece; participants, staging, triggers and outcomes. | Actors, actions, local conditions and location/level when spatial. |

A location is a fictional entity; a level is its playable treatment. A conversation encounter need not have traversal geometry. Start a blockout or paper walkthrough with placeholders after relevant capabilities exist. Finalization resolves participants, discovery and state, then checks playable staging. Observed constraints feed back to Core explicitly.

**Check:** Can the player reach and understand required options, learn essential information, and experience supported outcomes? Do not claim a paper review is evidence of an implemented playtest.
