# Art Direction

## Purpose and ownership

Keep visual treatments coherent and relevant information legible.

Silhouette/shape grammar, palette/contrast, materials, lighting, composition, visual hierarchy, differentiation, feedback and accessibility. Applies when visual work needs it.

## Questions

What must be recognized? What implies function, mood or allegiance? What remains readable under reduced visual information?

## Inputs and outputs

**Inputs:** Creative Direction and relevant world, setting, narrative and gameplay signal requirements.

**Outputs:** Visual grammar, priorities, differentiation rules and asset review tests.

## Prerequisites

Applicable fictional identity and signals; not a prerequisite for prose-only world records.

## Stability and evolution

**Keep stable:** Recognizable grammar and critical signals assumed across assets.

**Can evolve:** Bounded local motifs, palettes and techniques.

## Reusable rules

Separate decorative variation from functional cues. Critical distinctions must not rely solely on hue. Permit cultural variation within the grammar rather than making all assets identical.

## Acceptance test

Can distinct concepts be judged for coherence and relevant legibility without naming one as the rule?
