# Creative Direction

## Purpose and ownership

Keep the project recognizably itself across works and media.

Audience promise, emotional experience, themes, prioritized pillars, trade-off filters, scope and exclusions. Named protagonists and plot beats are Content.

## Questions

What should readers or players experience? What makes this project distinctive? Which attractive ideas do not belong? Which pillar wins when priorities conflict?

## Inputs and outputs

**Inputs:** Creator intent, audience, accessibility and production constraints, references, observations.

**Outputs:** A compact audience promise, choice filters, emotional range, priorities and non-goals.

## Prerequisites

No other Core prerequisite. Evidence from any medium can challenge the direction.

## Stability and evolution

**Keep stable:** Identity-defining promise, priorities and exclusions relied on by other domains.

**Can evolve:** Wording, examples and delivery methods within the promise. A changed priority requires impact review.

## Reusable rules

Phrase pillars as decision filters, not flattering adjectives. Separate desired experience from a particular UI, plot or mechanic. Record a meaningful exclusion and difficult trade-off.

## Acceptance test

Can competing content pitches be judged without turning either pitch into the shared rule?
