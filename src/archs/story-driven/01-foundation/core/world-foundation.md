# World Foundation

## Purpose and ownership

Establish a shared reality and bounded frame for places, peoples and conflicts.

Ontology, period/technology assumptions, spatial and temporal scale, cosmology when relevant, and ecological/social organizing forces. Specific regions and cities are Content.

## Questions

What kind of reality is this? What shapes ordinary life? What lies outside the story scope? What is intentionally unknown?

## Inputs and outputs

**Inputs:** Creative Direction, relevant research and feasibility constraints.

**Outputs:** World premise, organizing forces, scope and named unknowns.

## Prerequisites

Creative Direction.

## Stability and evolution

**Keep stable:** Reality assumptions and scale that downstream causality relies on.

**Can evolve:** Unexplored places and local details consistent with the frame.

## Reusable rules

Describe forces that can generate several distinct societies. Separate objective premises from inhabitants’ myths. Detail only the regions and institutions needed by the next slice.

## Acceptance test

Can two distinct regions satisfy the same world foundation?
