# Narrative Foundation

## Purpose and ownership

Define how authored meaning reaches an audience through voice, order, action and discovery.

Shared thematic/conflict tests, viewpoint, voice, continuity, exposition, revelation and pacing. Novel conventions include tense, distance and narrator reliability. Game conventions include agency, state and discovery order.

## Questions

What carries conflict and change? Who perceives and knows what? Which choices change what? How can the audience understand outcomes?

## Inputs and outputs

**Inputs:** Creative Direction and relevant world, setting and lore contracts; game actions/state only where interactive.

**Outputs:** Narrative contract, voice conventions, revelation/continuity tests and applicable agency rules.

## Prerequisites

Relevant shared rules. Coordinate game consequences with Game Design; prose does not depend on game or level rules.

## Stability and evolution

**Keep stable:** Viewpoint/voice promises, thematic boundaries, agency meaning and continuity conventions.

**Can evolve:** Specific plots, scene order and branch topology are Content and can evolve inside those rules.

## Reusable rules

Separate truth, testimony, character knowledge, audience knowledge and simulation state. A novel reveal can occur after the event. A game choice may affect expression, information, relationship, local outcome or global state; do not promise consequences for cosmetic branches. Essential understanding cannot depend on missed optional content.

## Acceptance test

Can both a linear chapter and an interactive scene be reviewed under applicable rules without prescribing their outcomes?
