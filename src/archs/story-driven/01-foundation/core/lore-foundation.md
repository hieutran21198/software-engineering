# Lore Foundation

## Purpose and ownership

Keep knowledge of history consistent without putting named historical events in Core.

Canon policy, time conventions, provenance, reliability, truth versus belief, intentional unknowns and contradiction handling.

## Questions

What is true, claimed, disputed or unknown? Who could know it? Can an account change without changing the event?

## Inputs and outputs

**Inputs:** World Foundation, Setting Rules and relevant mystery/tone aims.

**Outputs:** Truth/testimony vocabulary, chronology conventions, provenance and continuity tests.

## Prerequisites

World Foundation and Setting Rules.

## Stability and evolution

**Keep stable:** Meaning of claim labels and time conventions used by content.

**Can evolve:** New historical facts and interpretations are Content; policy revisions need impact review.

## Reusable rules

Distinguish established facts, in-world accounts, disputed claims and deliberate unknowns. Record time ordering and sources for historical claims. Keep reveal order separate from chronology and review maturity separate from truth.

## Acceptance test

Can two accounts disagree intentionally without both becoming objective canon?
