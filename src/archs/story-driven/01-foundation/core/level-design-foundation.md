# Level Design Foundation

## Purpose and ownership

Turn recurring spatial story and gameplay needs into usable principles.

Traversal/interaction metrics, routes, wayfinding, encounter grammar, teaching, pacing, checkpoints and spatial storytelling. Applies to spatial or encounter design.

## Questions

Can players reach and read intended options? How do spaces support verbs and story states? Where are rest, discovery and challenge possible?

## Inputs and outputs

**Inputs:** Applicable verbs/metrics, fictional limits, narrative constraints and visual cues.

**Outputs:** Spatial standards, flow/teaching patterns and walkthrough or playtest criteria.

## Prerequisites

Relevant Game, Narrative and Art contracts; world constraints for story-bearing places. Not required by every game.

## Stability and evolution

**Keep stable:** Traversal limits, affordances and critical navigation/readability assumptions.

**Can evolve:** Local layouts and encounter sequences are Content; changed shared metrics need impact review.

## Reusable rules

Derive reach, clearance and timing from player capability; test them rather than copying real-world dimensions blindly. Explain how required information is conveyed. Keep local beat sheets separate from shared pacing rules.

## Acceptance test

Can two levels be reviewed against the same rules without prescribing either layout?
