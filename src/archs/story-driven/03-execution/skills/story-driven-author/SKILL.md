# Story-Driven Author

## Required supporting skills

Read `../../../required-skills/kiss/SKILL.md` and `../../../required-skills/asd-ste-100/SKILL.md` from the adopted bundle. These utility resources are mounted from the repository utility sources during packaging. Apply them during existing self-checks to instructions and structured knowledge. Do not impose technical writing style on creative prose or dialogue. Resolve paths from this bundled skill directory; if a required resource is missing, report it rather than inventing its contents.

## Responsibility

Inspect the creative brief and available Core. Read relevant [Content guides](../../../01-foundation/content/README.md), then use the [Content](../../../04-templates/content-entry.md) and [work skeleton](../../../04-templates/work-skeleton.md) templates selectively. Start from existing material when supplied and preserve its authoritative facts.

Build a linked premise, motivations, opposition, stakes, causal progression and meaningful outcomes. A catalogue of places or characters is not a story skeleton. Do not mandate one act structure or character formula. Identify scene contributions and resulting changes; reflective and atmospheric scenes can be useful.

Place particular identity and historical facts in World; arcs and changes in Narrative; playable treatment in Gameplay; concepts in Visual; placements in Level. Link records rather than copying shared canon. Scope a work or playthrough outcome explicitly.

For novels, follow applicable viewpoint, tense, voice, knowledge and reveal rules; gameplay, art and level design are not mandatory inputs. For games, map agency to supported actions/state/consequences, cover required understanding without optional lore, and include environment/system delivery when useful. A paper walkthrough is not an implemented playtest.

Self-check using [validation](../../../02-methodology/validation.md). Mark unresolved assumptions, intentional mysteries and missing prerequisites. Return shared-rule conflicts to the architect rather than inventing exceptions. Creator approval is needed before provisional dependent content is called final. Report linked records, the outline, review findings and remaining questions; creative prose and dialogue retain the project's voice.
