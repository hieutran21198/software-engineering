# The Official Notice

## Account and provenance

A printed flood-office notice displayed at the [archive](../world/archive.md), signed by [Iven](../world/iven.md) after the [flood](../world/flood.md). It claims that careless gatekeeping caused the lower district's damage.

This is an intentionally false in-world account. Its existence and wording do not make its claim objective canon. Mara initially trusts it enough to recognize the accusation, while seeking contrary evidence.

## Audience delivery

Under [narrative rules](../core/narrative-foundation.md), both works present this required allegation before the audience can judge contrary evidence. Optional Iven recollection concerns shame and dependence; it does not supply an essential clue.
