# Novel Skeleton: The Sealed Archive

## Work brief

A short prose investigation for readers interested in moral uncertainty. Scope: one archive visit and its immediate decision. Use the five shared contracts, especially [direction](../core/creative-direction.md), [narrative conventions](../core/narrative-foundation.md), [access rules](../core/setting-rules.md) and [canon policy](../core/lore-foundation.md). No Game, Art or Level input is required.

## Conflict and composition

Use the [arc](arc.md) and linked [Mara](../world/mara.md), [Iven](../world/iven.md), [place](../world/archive.md) and [event](../world/flood.md). Viewpoint is close third-person past tense through Mara. The record's existence predates the story; its contents are revealed later.

| Chapter/scene | Purpose and entry knowledge | Resulting change and reveal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1: The public page | Mara reads the [notice](notice.md); she remembers evacuation but not the order. The reader knows the allegation, not the truth. | She requests original evidence, shifting from private doubt to investigation. |
| 1: The signature | Iven evades, then explains certification risks after Mara asks for access. The visible water mark gives damage a physical trace. | He signs inspection authorization. Mara recognizes his fear without gaining access to his unspoken thoughts. |
| 2: The order | Supervised inspection permits Mara to compare the [ledger](../world/ledger.md) against the notice. | She and the reader learn the documented order; Iven's account remains limited to what he saw. She requests a certified copy; Iven separately signs its certification. |
| 2: A bounded correction | Mara holds the certified copy and understands disclosure risks. | She submits it under seal to the civic court. The authored ending opens an inquiry without pretending a verdict or universal vindication already exists. |

## Handoff and review

Draft dialogue and prose within the chosen voice, without a required technical-writing style. Each scene changes goal, access, knowledge or commitment. Iven's optional recollection can deepen empathy but is not needed to understand evidence or the ending. Legal results and council motives are deliberately unresolved; a later chapter would need new content rather than inventing them in the manuscript silently.
