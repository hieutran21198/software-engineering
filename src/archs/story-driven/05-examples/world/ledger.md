# Original Flood Ledger

## Fictional identity

A sealed municipal ledger with original official marks, recording the diversion order and execution described by the [flood record](flood.md). It documents an institutional action, not all private motives. It has no supernatural capability.

## Custody and use

Stored at the [archive](archive.md); [Iven](iven.md) can authorize inspection and separately certify a copy. Apply [record/access rules](../core/setting-rules.md) and [provenance policy](../core/lore-foundation.md).

The novel's discovery and the game's [ledger treatment](../gameplay/ledger.md) reference this identity. Discovery does not make the player the owner of the original.
