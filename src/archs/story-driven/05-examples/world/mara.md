# Mara

## Identity and authoritative facts

Mara is a 25-year-old records apprentice from Greybank. She was ten during the flood and remembers evacuation, not the office's decision process. Her father was publicly accused of careless gatekeeping. She wants his reputation corrected and fears that her need for certainty will make her dismiss inconvenient witnesses.
