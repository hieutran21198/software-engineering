# Iven

## Identity and authoritative facts

Iven is the archive's custodian. During the flood he worked in the flood office and later signed the public account. He saw the written diversion order and knows the public notice's central allegation is false. He did not witness the council's deliberations and cannot know each member's private motive.

He wants to preserve the archive and protect his dependent household from retaliation. Shame makes him evade questions; it does not give him omniscient knowledge.
