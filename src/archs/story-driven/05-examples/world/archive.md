# Greybank Archive

## Fictional place

Greybank is a river town. Its municipal archive stores sealed flood-office records; the town council controls funding, while a separate civic court can hear a petition. [Iven](iven.md) is its custodian. Inspection takes place at a supervised reading desk.

## Applicable constraints

Use [world institutions](../core/world-foundation.md) and [access/copying rules](../core/setting-rules.md). Required records are secured; a publicly displayed [notice](../narrative/notice.md) and a water stain at the reading-room entrance provide accessible evidence. The stain's marked year connects it to the [flood](flood.md), but proves no actor's motive.

This is fictional identity, not playable geometry. The [confrontation](../level/confrontation.md) provides a conversation staging brief; an actual navigable level would need additional Level and Art contracts.
