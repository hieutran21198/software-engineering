# Narrative Foundation: Evidence Before Judgment

## Shared rules

Use the [creative promise](creative-direction.md), [setting constraints](setting-rules.md) and [canon policy](lore-foundation.md). Understanding the central allegation and contrary evidence is essential; motives may remain partly unknown. Reveal facts through what participants can perceive or learn. Deliberate ambiguity concerns responsibility and cost, not whether a required clue was ever conveyed.

## Novel applicability

Use close third-person past tense through Mara. Do not narrate Iven's unspoken knowledge before Mara learns it. Voice is restrained and observant; technical writing utilities do not prescribe the prose. Delay the original order until the reader has encountered the official account.

## Game applicability

Agency concerns information and the disclosure outcome. Finding evidence and choosing disclosure have supported state effects. Essential evidence is required before the decision; optional recollection adds empathy, not comprehension. No combat or traversal promise is made by this slice.

## Checks

Chronology can differ from reading/play order, but knowledge cannot jump without evidence. Public release and restricted inquiry are local authored outcomes, not changes to shared past events. Coordinate outcomes with [game state rules](game-design-foundation.md) only for the game.
