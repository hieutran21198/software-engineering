# Lore Foundation: Events and Accounts

## Applicability and inputs

Shared across both works; use the [world frame](world-foundation.md) and [record constraints](setting-rules.md).

## Conventions

Use civic years and causal ordering for dated events. Label objective teaching-example facts separately from in-world claims. Identify the source and who could know each claim. A character's intentional lie remains a lie even when the account has been reviewed.

An unknown motive remains unknown. The author may reveal documentary evidence of an order without providing an omniscient explanation of every council member's intention. Presentation order is not historical order.

## Check

Can the original record and a public notice disagree without both being treated as the event itself? Record an actual event in World and its public account in Narrative.
