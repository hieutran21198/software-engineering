# World Foundation: Civic River Towns

## Frame and applicability

Shared fiction uses river settlements, paper municipal records, print notices and civic courts independent of local flood offices. Officials can be fallible or corrupt; witnesses have limited knowledge. There is no magic or technology that reconstructs the past infallibly.

## Inputs and outputs

The [creative promise](creative-direction.md) constrains the scale to a local human conflict. The output is a world frame in which records and living testimony matter; it does not name a particular town's history.

## Limits and checks

Evidence can establish a recorded action, not every actor's private intention. New regions can differ while preserving those limits. Check proposed investigative shortcuts against this frame and [Setting Rules](setting-rules.md).
