# Encounter: The Disclosure Decision

## Staging and applicability

A conversation/evidence encounter at the [archive](../world/archive.md) between [Mara](../world/mara.md) and [Iven](../world/iven.md). This is a placed narrative interaction, not a traversable level. It needs [Narrative](../core/narrative-foundation.md) and [Game](../core/game-design-foundation.md) rules; no movement metrics are assumed.

## Entry and contribution

Entry requires evidence_compared and copy_certified. The [quest](../narrative/game.md) owns outcomes and the [ledger treatment](../gameplay/ledger.md) owns evidence interaction. The encounter presents each disclosure option with its consequences before commitment. Iven's optional recollection can be absent; required risk explanations are always presented.

## Environmental delivery

The dated water mark and displayed [notice](../narrative/notice.md) connect past damage to official testimony. They provide context, not proof of hidden motives. A later visual treatment would require Art rules for clear evidence cues; a navigable archive would require Level rules and a geometry record.

## Review

Paper staging makes the essential decision available and understandable without optional dialogue. It does not claim geometry, accessibility or player behavior has been playtested.
