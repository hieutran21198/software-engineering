# Creative Brief: Work or Slice Name

## Immediate objective

What useful result should this authoring session produce? Identify novel, game or both, audience, intended experience, production constraints and included/excluded scope.

## Existing inputs

List supplied material and authoritative knowledge references. Distinguish facts, creator decisions, exploratory ideas and available approvals.

## Decisions and questions

Record important assumptions, unresolved preferences, intentional mysteries and the few questions that materially change the next slice. Do not repeat questions answered by existing knowledge.

## Next slice and review

Name the representative conflict/sequence to develop, required inputs and the review method. Identify which decisions need creator review. Move reusable direction into its owning Core record rather than maintaining it twice here.
