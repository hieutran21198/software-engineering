# Core Contract: Domain Name

## Purpose, owner and applicability

Name the domain and what it governs. State whether each rule applies project-wide, to a medium, or to a work. Keep named instances in Content.

## Inputs and prerequisites

Link relevant contracts and explain what sufficiently defined input is required. Distinguish prerequisites from feedback and optional inputs.

## Rules and limits

State reusable decisions, conditions, effects, costs, exclusions and exception criteria. Define the recognizable promise or convention where a causal formula would be inappropriate.

## Contradiction tests

Explain how different proposed content entries can be checked against the rules. Record stable assumptions and what can evolve within them.

## Unknowns and revision impact

Identify provisional decisions and intentional unknowns. For a revision, record its reason, affected contracts/content and review findings. Preserve creator approval through available conversation or Git; do not add a workflow status database.
